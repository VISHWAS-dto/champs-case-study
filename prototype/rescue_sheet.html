Late-Demo Rescue Sheet

Flags demos booked more than 48h after the lead arrived, so reps can offer a slot before created_at + 48h. Your data stays in this browser; nothing is uploaded.

Load a CRM export and press Build today's list.

Build a list first.

Weekly readout: join rate (J/S) of late demos, rescue arm vs control. Uses the export loaded above (load a later export with demo_joined filled in) and the saved outcomes. Intent-to-treat: every late demo counts in its arm, whatever the outcome.

Start of shift (analyst)

  1. Export scheduled demos from the CRM (same 12 columns as the case file).
  2. Pick it under 1. CRM export. Add yesterday's rescue_outcomes.csv under 2 if you have one.
  3. Leave Run as of at now (for the case data use 2026-07-15 09:00) and press Build today's list.
  4. Share the page, or Download rescue list into the shift's sheet.

During shift (rep)

  1. Pick your rep ID. Work MOVE rows top-down: offer a slot before the deadline shown.
  2. CONFIRM rows are past their deadline: just confirm the current slot live.
  3. Pick one outcome per row. It saves automatically.

Rules (in rescue_logic.js → buildRescueList)

  • Flag: demo booked, gap from lead creation > threshold, demo still in the future, lead created before "as of".
  • Deadline = created_at + threshold. MOVE if it has not passed, else CONFIRM.
  • Arm: last digit of lead ID odd → rescue (shown to reps), even → control (never shown, never called).

Known limits

  • The export clock is assumed to be UTC. Only the displayed local times depend on this; confirm the time with the parent on the call.
  • Outcomes are saved in this browser only. If reps log on different machines, download each log and merge them.
  • The file sample/rescue_outcomes_demo.csv is SIMULATED, only to demo the tabs. It is not evidence.
