Late-Demo Rescue Sheet

Flags upcoming demos whose slot is more than 48h after lead creation, so reps can offer a slot before created_at + 48h. Your data stays in this browser; nothing is uploaded.

Load a CRM export and press Build today's list.

Build a list first.

Weekly readout: join rate (J/S), rescue arm vs control. Uses the export loaded above (load a later export with demo_joined filled in), the saved outcomes and the first-seen register this browser keeps each time a list is built. Intent-to-treat: every listed late demo counts in its arm, whatever the outcome. Primary: the MOVE-eligible stratum (deadline still ≥3h away when the row was first listed). Decision rule: scale if the CI's lower end > 0; stop only if its upper end < +3 pp; otherwise extend.

Start of shift (analyst)

  1. Export scheduled demos from the CRM (same 12 columns as the case file).
  2. Pick it under 1. CRM export. Add yesterday's rescue_outcomes.csv under 2 if you have one.
  3. Leave Run as of at now and press Build today's list. Build the list on the same browser every day: it keeps the first-seen register the Measurement tab needs.
  4. Share the page, or Download rescue list into the shift's sheet.

During shift (rep)

  1. Pick your rep ID. Work MOVE rows top-down: offer a slot before the deadline shown.
  2. CONFIRM rows have less than 3h left or are past their deadline: just confirm the current slot live.
  3. Pick one outcome per row. It saves automatically.

Rules (in rescue_logic.js → buildRescueList)

  • Flag: demo booked, demo slot more than the threshold after lead creation, demo still in the future, lead created before "as of".
  • Deadline = created_at + threshold. MOVE if at least 3h remain, else CONFIRM.
  • Arm: last digit of lead ID odd → rescue (shown to reps), even → control (never shown, never called).

Known limits

  • The export clock is assumed to be UTC. Only the displayed local times depend on this; confirm the time with the parent on the call.
  • Outcomes are saved in this browser only. If reps log on different machines, download each log and merge them.
  • Load sample data uses SYNTHETIC leads and SIMULATED outcomes (sample/), only to demo the tabs. It is not the case data and not evidence.
  • Clear saved outcomes also clears the first-seen register.
