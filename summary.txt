- BrightChamps loses possible customer

1. The Leak   - where the most money is lost, in rupees per month.
2. The Lever  - one fix that can go live in 2 weeks with no engineering work,
                plus the other ideas we rejected and why.
3. The Build  - a small working tool that really runs.
4. The Memo   - a one-page PDF summing it all up.

Key numbers given:
- Rs 60,000 = money earned from each customer who pays.
- Rs 900    = marketing cost of getting each lead.
