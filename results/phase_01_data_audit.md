## 7. Timezone handling

- Neither timestamp column carries a timezone or UTC offset.
- The assignment PDF does not say which clock the timestamps are recorded in (UTC, IST, server time, or the parent's local time). **This cannot be determined from the provided materials.**
- `parent_timezone` values are all valid IANA names, so local-time conversion is *possible* once the source clock is assumed.
- Observation only: in every geography the stored hours are spread almost evenly across the day (median ~12, IQR ~6–18, close to what a uniform 0–23 spread gives), with no day/night pattern. The hour of day therefore gives no evidence about which clock is used.
